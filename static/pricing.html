Pricing

Pay per call. No subscription, no seat fees, no monthly minimum.

PRICE:$0.0005–$29.99 per call (90% of tools <= $0.003)
RAILS:x402 / MPP / balance
MINIMUM:$0

Per-call price ranges

Every tool has a fixed, published USD price, set from the real upstream cost plus margin. There is no plan, no included quota, no overage fee — every call, at any volume, costs exactly its listed price.

Per-audio-minute tools

transcribe.submit (speech-to-text) is priced per audio minute: max($0.01, ceil(minutes) × $0.005), rounded up to a cent — 1 minute $0.01, 10 minutes $0.05, 30 minutes $0.15, 61 minutes $0.31. You declare duration_seconds (required, up to 4 hours); the 402 quote shows the computed price and the job is cut at the declared duration.

Cache discount

A tool response is cached per its own TTL (5 seconds for volatile data like crypto prices, up to 7 days for near-static data). A cache hit within that window costs 10% of the listed price — billed via balance debit, no on-chain settlement needed for a hit.

The 10% discount applies on the Balance rail only. x402 and MPP payments are verified and settled against the full listed price before the call runs, so a cache hit on either of those rails is still billed at 100% — there is no on-chain equivalent of a post-hoc discount once a payment has already settled.

Integrator orders

Integrator: 0% (pilot) fee from the merchant, min $0.05, orders from $1.00; on Tempo inside the transaction, on Base inside the transaction for clients that support fee-split and invoiced otherwise.

Integrator fee: 0% (pilot). Fee Switch state is published here per Merchant Agreement §8.

Orders placed through a merchant storefront are priced by the merchant, not by the tool table above. Details: /integrator.

What "no hidden costs" actually means

  • No subscription tier, no seat count, no annual contract.
  • No overage fee — the price per call is the price per call at any volume.
  • No markup surprise at settlement — the price is locked at the moment of the HTTP 402 challenge, not renegotiated after the call runs.
  • A failed provider call is never billed — only a delivered response is charged.

Why we publish this page

Most aggregators in this space either hide pricing behind a "contact sales" form or don't publish a pricing page at all — we checked, and as of this writing the closest direct competitor's /pricing URL returns a live 404. A platform whose entire pitch is machine-native, pay-per-call payments should be able to show its prices on a page. This is that page.
