Partners that sell for you

A partner permission lets a marketplace you allowed upload your catalog and receive your order events for your store. Stopping it takes one free wallet signature, no gas; it does not close your store and does not touch your payouts or orders.

Technical details

With the merchant key the same stop is DELETE /api/v1/shop/merchants/me/grants/<partner> or the MCP tool shop.merchant.grant_revoke. This page signs the message of purpose grant_revoke and sends it to POST /api/v1/shop/merchants/me/grants/<partner>/revoke. See Partner grants.
