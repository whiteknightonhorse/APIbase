Use case

Tools for company research

Official-registry company lookups across four jurisdictions, behind one endpoint — no per-registry signup.

Live tools

ToolJurisdictionPriceWhat it returns
edgar.company_searchUnited States (SEC EDGAR)$0.001Search public companies by name, ticker, or keyword — company name, CIK number, form type, filing date
edgar.company_factsUnited States (SEC EDGAR)$0.001XBRL financials by CIK — revenue, net income, assets, liabilities, equity, EPS, last 5 reporting periods per metric
edgar.filingsUnited States (SEC EDGAR)$0.001Recent filings by CIK — 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, proxy statements, with document URLs
brreg.searchNorway (Brønnøysundregistrene)$0.001Search by company name, municipality, org form, or NACE code — org number, VAT status, employee count, address
brasilapi.cnpjBrazil$0.002Lookup by 14-digit CNPJ tax ID — name, address, CNAE codes, partners (QSA)
abr.abn_lookupAustralia$0.001Resolve an Australian Business Number to full entity details

All six are no-auth on the upstream side (public registries) — the live, always-current entries are at /api/v1/tools (filter with ?category=finance or ?category=business) or via GET /api/v1/discover?intent=company+research.

How to call it

curl -X POST https://apibase.pro/api/v1/tools/edgar.company_search/call \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ak_live_..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"params": {"query": "Tesla"}}'

Not authenticated yet? /connect covers getting a key, or paying directly from a wallet with no key at all.

What this covers today

Four jurisdictions, not a global company database — a company outside the US, Norway, Brazil, or Australia isn't reachable through these specific tools yet. Each tool queries its own country's official registry directly; there is no cross-jurisdiction merge or entity-resolution step, so results are exactly what that one registry returns.
