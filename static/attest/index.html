Attest an APIbase receipt on GenLayer

Attestations are produced by GenLayer validators, each of which fetches this receipt and the USDC transfer on Base independently; APIbase cannot alter the result.

v0 limits: only x402 calls settled on Base, and only billed calls, have a receipt that can be attested. Calls paid from a prepaid balance or on Tempo are not covered yet.

Receipt

Open this page as /attest/?r=<receipt id>, using the receipt URL https://apibase.pro/r/<receipt id>.json returned with a paid call.

Attestations on GenLayer

Contract on GenLayer testnet Bradbury (chain 4221). Existing attestations are read directly from the Bradbury RPC; no wallet is needed to read them.

Attest this receipt

Connect your wallet on Bradbury (chain 4221) and call attest(receipt_id). You need GEN for gas: get it from the GenLayer faucet. The transaction is signed in your own wallet; this page holds no key.
