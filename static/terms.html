1. Service Description

APIbase.pro ("Service") is an MCP (Model Context Protocol) gateway that provides AI agents with programmatic access to 1402 tools from 405 providers through a single endpoint. The Service includes domain registration, DNS management, data retrieval, content generation, and other tools. Payments are processed via x402 (USDC on Base), MPP (USDC on Tempo), or a prepaid balance, as listed on the pricing page. Merchant orders: see the Merchant Agreement.

2. Acceptance

By accessing or using the Service, you agree to these Terms. If you are using the Service on behalf of an organization, you represent that you have authority to bind that organization. If you use the Service through an autonomous software agent, the agent acts with your authority; its tool calls, payments and orders bind you, and you are responsible for orders it places by mistake.

3. Agent Accounts

  • Agents receive API keys automatically upon first request (auto-registration).
  • Each agent is identified by a unique agent_id and API key hash.
  • You are responsible for safeguarding your API key. Do not share it.
  • All activity under your API key is your responsibility.

4. Payment Terms

  • Tool calls are billed per call via x402 (USDC on Base), MPP (USDC on Tempo), or a prepaid balance, exactly the rails listed on the pricing page.
  • Prices are listed in the tool catalog.
  • Escrow model (platform tool calls only): USDC is locked before the provider call. On failure, USDC is auto-refunded.
  • Merchant orders: see the Merchant Agreement.
  • Domain registration fees include the upstream registrar cost plus a service markup.
  • All payments are final. Refunds are automatic on provider failure only.

5. Prohibited Uses

The following activities are strictly prohibited. Violations result in immediate account suspension and may be reported to law enforcement.

This list applies to platform tool calls. Goods sold through merchant storefronts follow the single list in the Acceptable Use Policy section 2 (machine-readable at /legal/prohibited-categories.json); where both apply the stricter rule wins.

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — zero tolerance. Any content exploiting, depicting, or sexualizing minors. Reported to NCMEC and law enforcement immediately.
  • Illegal drugs and controlled substances — sale, distribution, or promotion of narcotics, steroids, drug paraphernalia, or products designed to pass drug tests.
  • Weapons and explosives — sale or promotion of firearms, ammunition, explosives, or instructions to manufacture weapons.
  • Human trafficking and organ trade — any content related to trafficking, exploitation, or illegal trade of human beings or organs.
  • Terrorism and violent extremism — recruitment, glorification, planning, or promotion of terrorist acts or extremist ideologies.
  • Fraud and phishing — impersonation, identity theft, credential harvesting, or financial fraud schemes.
  • Malware and hacking — distribution of viruses, ransomware, spyware, or tools for unauthorized access to systems.
  • Harassment and hate speech — content promoting hatred or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin. Automated pre-call filtering for this category is in development; report suspected violations to support for manual review.
  • Self-harm and suicide — content promoting, encouraging, or providing instructions for self-harm or suicide.
  • Counterfeit goods — sale or promotion of fake branded products or forged documents (IDs, passports, diplomas).
  • Spam and abuse — mass automated requests intended to degrade service, DDoS, scraping beyond fair use, or abusing tool calls for non-intended purposes.
  • Domains for illegal purposes — domains registered through our Service must not be used for any of the prohibited activities listed above.

See our Moderation Policy for how automated content review works, what happens when a paid call is blocked, how to appeal, and exactly what content we retain.

7. Audit Trail and Compliance

  • Financial records are stored in an append-only ledger (never modified or deleted).
  • We comply with lawful data requests from law enforcement authorities.
  • Nginx access logs (including IP addresses) are retained for 15 days.

14. Integrator: orders from merchants

This section applies to orders placed through a merchant storefront (/mcp/m/<slug>).

APIbase is a technology intermediary; buyer funds do not pass through APIbase; the fee is charged to the merchant; APIbase has no registered legal entity and is operated as a decentralised autonomous organisation of independent open-source developers.

  • Who sells. A buyer's agent buys from the merchant, not from APIbase. APIbase is not the seller and is not a payment institution.
  • Payment. The buyer pays USDC directly to the merchant's wallet, at exactly the amount of the quote.
  • Two payment models. Platform tool calls (sections 1–4) are billed by APIbase per call. Merchant orders are paid to the merchant; the fee is charged to the merchant: 0% during the pilot, minimum $0.05 per order once enabled; on Tempo it is taken in the same transaction, on Base it is invoiced to the merchant.
  • Disputes. Refunds, returns and disputes are between the buyer and the merchant under the merchant's published policy and the law of the merchant's country. APIbase records them and is not an arbitrator. See Refund Framework.
  • Operator. APIbase has no registered legal entity; it is operated as a decentralised autonomous organisation of independent open-source developers, identified by the domain apibase.pro and the public repository github.com/whiteknightonhorse/APIbase. Contact: support@apibase.pro.
  • Agent orders. An order placed by your agent binds you. On-chain order payments are irreversible; refunds follow the merchant's published policy and the Refund Framework.
  • Subscriptions and streams. Renewal only inside the published window and only for the authorised amount; cancel any time, effective at period end; streams settle consumed seconds only and return the unconsumed deposit. Terms: Merchant Agreement section 10.
  • Merchant documents. Merchant Agreement, Acceptable Use Policy, DPA.
