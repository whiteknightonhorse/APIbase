About

APIbase.pro is an MCP gateway: one endpoint that gives AI agents access to a catalog of third-party API tools, with pay-per-call pricing. The Integrator lets online stores accept payments from AI agents.

Questions, corrections and takedown requests: contact page.

AI-assisted content

Some pages on this site, in particular the guides, are written and reviewed with the help of AI. Facts about APIbase itself (fees, minimum order, payment rails, prices) are not written by the AI: they are inserted from the live product data and checked automatically before a page is published. No page claims to have been reviewed by a human editor. Editorial responsibility for all content rests with APIbase.
