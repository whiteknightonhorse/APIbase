## New — Integrator

AI payment for merchants. A merchant gets its own MCP storefront (`POST https://apibase.pro/mcp/m/<slug>`); a buyer's agent gets a quote and pays in USDC on one of two rails: x402 on Base or MPP on Tempo.

- **Non-custodial.** `pay_to` is the merchant's payout wallet; APIbase never holds the money.
- **Fee: 0% during the pilot.**
- **Connect by agent or by hand.** Merchant sign-up is a wallet signature plus acceptance of four versioned legal documents. Start at the [overview](https://apibase.pro/integrator) or the [agent guide](https://apibase.pro/integrator/agent-guide).
- **Catalog three ways.** API, CSV import, or the WooCommerce plugin (`packages/woocommerce-apibase`).
- **Quote, then pay** over x402 (Base) or MPP (Tempo), with `payment-identifier` idempotency.
- **End-to-end encrypted buyer data.** Plaintext is rejected; data is purged after 7 days.
- **Verified on-chain refunds** and payer disputes with a 7-day window.
- **Subscriptions.** Tempo; on Base, pull by payer pre-authorization.
- **Streaming sessions** with a merchant-side settler CLI (`packages/stream-settler`).
- **Signed webhooks** plus event pull.
- **Catalog moderation** and a connection check ([demo](https://apibase.pro/integrator/check/apibase-demo)).
- Platform guides: [WooCommerce](https://apibase.pro/integrator/platforms/woocommerce), [Shopify](https://apibase.pro/integrator/platforms/shopify), [Tilda](https://apibase.pro/integrator/platforms/tilda), [Wix](https://apibase.pro/integrator/platforms/wix), [custom site](https://apibase.pro/integrator/platforms/custom).
- Live: [demo shop](https://apibase.pro/m/apibase-demo), [shop directory](https://apibase.pro/shops), [machine-readable summary](https://apibase.pro/integrator/llms.txt).

Legal documents v1.2 (merchant agreement, acceptable use, DPA, refund framework), accepted by wallet signature: [index](https://apibase.pro/legal/index.json).

## Verified with real money

Checked live with real USDC before release (verification journals, 2026-10-12):

- **Connection.** A fresh merchant was connected by an agent starting from an empty session in 2 min 45 s, and was paid $0.01 straight to its wallet.
- **Buyer data.** Encrypted flow with 0 leaks of the canary values; plaintext rejected.
- **Refund.** On-chain refund tx dce970e7b3e89f6aae325ad86486004ccb2ba69f73d356f3cf98d7d4e269d837.
- **Base subscription.** Period 1 paid, `payment-identifier` replay returned the same transaction (one confirmed payment), and the same id on another quote returned `payment_identifier_conflict`.
- **Tempo subscription.** Periods 1 and 2 paid and fulfilled.
- **WooCommerce.** WooCommerce 11.2.1 on WordPress 7.1.3: plugin sync, then a $1 order paid on Base (tx fcf21ca6ac883f175814b7bfc63d273c1602f38f0300222f12630053078048b3), fulfilled by webhook, Woo order completed.
- **Streaming session.** Three 60 s chunks (cumulative $0.018), closed on-chain by the merchant's settler CLI (Tempo close tx cac2b6a5dc8dd8631445ded9c34c5cd86e569c11905712f6a1ec375ca8896c24); the CLI reported "pass: 1 reported, 0 failed" and the server marked the channel closed and verified. Two fixes made this pass: the close proof (T-0304) and the settler waiting for the receipt before reporting (T-0307).
