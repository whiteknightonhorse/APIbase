QUOTED ──(TTL)──► EXPIRED
  │  └─(cancel)─► CANCELLED
  ▼ pay presented
PAYING ──(verify/settle fail)──► PAYMENT_FAILED (quote stays open until TTL)
  │                                 │  └─(repeat payment of a live quote)──► PAYING
  │                                 └─(reconcile: chain confirmed by (payer, nonce))──► PAID
  ▼ on-chain confirmed
PAID ──(merchant confirm | auto for instant)──► CONFIRMED
  │  └─(cancel by buyer before CONFIRMED, grace)─► REFUND_PENDING
  ▼
CONFIRMED ──(instant: fulfillment stored)──► FULFILLED ──► CLOSED
          ──(physical: ship)──► SHIPPED ──► DELIVERED ──(refund_window)──► CLOSED
Any of PAID…DELIVERED:
  ──(refund request)──► REFUND_PENDING ──(merchant tx verified)──► REFUNDED (full) | PARTIALLY_REFUNDED → continues the path
  ──(dispute)──► DISPUTED ──► (resolved_*) → returns to the previous state or REFUND_PENDING
